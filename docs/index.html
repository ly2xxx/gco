GCO 高尔夫球会

GCO Golf Club

GCO 是一群生活在苏格兰格拉斯哥一带、热爱高尔夫的华人球友组成的业余社群。我们在高尔夫的故乡结伴打球、切磋球技、结交朋友，把每一场周末球局串成一个有积分、有排名、也有故事的完整赛季。

GCO is an amateur community of Chinese golfers living in and around Glasgow. We play the courses of the home of golf together, swap tips, make friends, and turn our weekend rounds into a full season of league, cup and team golf.

我们怎么打球How we play

🏆

个人联赛League

6 轮 18 洞比杆赛，按净杆积分排出年度 Order of Merit。

Six 18-hole stroke-play rounds, ranked on net score.

🥊

个人杯赛Cup

12 人抽签单淘汰比洞赛，一洞一洞决出杯赛冠军。

A 12-player knockout match-play draw.

🤝

红黑对抗赛Team match

红队 vs 黑队，双人赛与四人 Scramble 争夺团队奖杯。

Red vs Black in fourball and scramble formats.

🏅

年终总决赛Finals

联赛前三与杯赛冠军 36 洞比杆，决出年度总冠军。

The league's top three and the cup winner play 36 holes for the title.

我们的约定What we value

遵守 R&A 高尔夫规则和球场 local rules，注意打球节奏，修理球痕、耙平沙坑，尊重对手和同组球友。最重要的是 —— Have fun!

We play by the R&A Rules of Golf, keep up the pace, repair pitch marks, rake bunkers and respect every playing partner. Above all, we have fun.
