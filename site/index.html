GCO 高尔夫球会

GCO 是一个以华人球友为主的业余高尔夫社群。我们以球会友，在苏格兰格拉斯哥一带的球场切磋球技、结交朋友，也在微信群里分享每一轮的成绩与故事。

GCO is an amateur golf community of Chinese golfers based around Glasgow, Scotland — friends who meet on the course to compete, improve and enjoy the game together.

我们怎么打 How we play

🏆 个人联赛 League

6 轮 18 洞比杆赛，按净杆数累计积分。

Six rounds of 18-hole stroke play, ranked on net score.

🥊 个人杯赛 Cup

抽签单淘汰比洞赛，一对一决出杯赛冠军。

A drawn knockout match-play bracket, settled head to head.

🔴⚫ 红黑对抗 Team Match

红黑两队各 6 人，双人赛与四人赛争夺团队奖杯。

Red vs Black, six a side, in pairs and four-man scramble events.

🏅 年终总决赛 Season Final

联赛前三与杯赛冠军 36 洞比杆，决出年度总冠军。

The league's top three and the cup winner play 36 holes for the title.

遵守 R&A 高尔夫规则与球场礼仪：修球痕、耙沙坑、不慢打，尊重每一位对手。

We play by the R&A Rules of Golf and look after the course and each other. Have fun!
