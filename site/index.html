GCO 高尔夫球会

格拉斯哥华人高尔夫社区 · A Chinese golf community in Glasgow, Scotland

关于我们 About us

GCO 是一群生活在苏格兰格拉斯哥及周边地区、热爱高尔夫的华人球友。我们在高尔夫的故乡以球会友，每个赛季一起打联赛、杯赛和红黑团队对抗赛，在球场上切磋球技，在球场外结下友谊。

GCO is a group of Chinese golfers living in and around Glasgow. Here in the home of golf we play a full season together, from April to September, with a league, a knockout cup and a Red vs Black team match. The scores matter, but the friendships made on the course matter more.

赛季赛事 The season

🏆

个人联赛 League

6 轮 18 洞比杆赛，按净杆积分排名。
Six rounds of stroke play, ranked on net score.

🥊

个人杯赛 Cup

12 人抽签单淘汰比洞赛。
A 12-player knockout match-play draw.

🤝

团队赛 Team Match

红队 vs 黑队，三场双人赛加一场四人赛争夺团队奖杯。
Red vs Black over three pairs matches and a four-man scramble.

🥇

年终总决赛 Grand Final

36 洞比杆赛决出赛季总冠军。
36 holes of stroke play to crown the champion.

赛季成绩、公告和赛程由 GCO 赛事看板管理。
Scores, announcements and fixtures are kept in the GCO dashboard.
